# Judge rubric

Three yes/no questions about one claim and the quote it rests on. This rubric has not been calibrated yet: it changes only through a calibration run (Suite J).

1. **supported**: Does the quote, on its own, support the claim? Answer no when the claim says something the quote does not say, names a material, condition or comparison the quote does not name, or reverses a comparison.
2. **certainty_kept**: Is the claim no more certain than the quote? "Suggests", "may", "could" and "appears to" must not become "shows", "proves" or "is". A result under one condition must not become a general result. Answer no when the claim is stronger than the quote.
3. **numbers_match**: Is every number in the claim in the quote with the same value and unit? Answer yes when the claim has no numbers. Answer no when a number is changed, rounded to a different value or missing from the quote.

Give a reason of one line that names what is wrong, or "all three hold".
